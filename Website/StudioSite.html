About Sarah

A photographer who listens

I'm Sarah Kim — a New York-based photographer with 12 years of experience capturing weddings, portraits, and corporate events. I believe great photography starts with genuine connection.

My approach is relaxed and documentary — I'm not directing a photoshoot, I'm documenting real moments as they unfold. The result is images that feel authentic, emotional, and timeless.
